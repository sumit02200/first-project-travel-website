About the Maldives

The Maldives is a stunning tropical nation in the Indian Ocean composed of 26 ring-shaped atolls, made up of more than 1,000 coral islands. It's known for its beaches, blue lagoons, and extensive reefs — making it one of the most beautiful travel destinations in the world.
