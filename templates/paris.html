About Paris

Paris, the capital of France, is one of the world’s most visited cities. Known for its art, fashion, gastronomy, and culture, Paris offers stunning architecture, charming cafés, and landmarks like the Eiffel Tower and the Louvre Museum. Whether you stroll along the Seine or explore Montmartre, every corner of Paris tells a story.
